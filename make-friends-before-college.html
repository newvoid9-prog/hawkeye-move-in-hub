Before move-in

How to make friends before college starts

Almost every incoming freshman worries about showing up alone. The months before move-in are actually the easiest time of the year to meet people, because everyone in your class is looking at the same time.

By Maya Lindqvist · Updated October 27, 2026 · 6 min read

  2. Comment first
  3. Pick the right group chats

Key takeaways

  • Post a specific intro on your class page. Specific gets DMs; generic gets scrolled past.
  • Small group chats beat giant ones.
  • Your roommate search doubles as a friend search.
  • Online friends only become real friends when you make a plan for week one.

1. Get on your class Instagram page

Most schools have a "Class of 2031" page run by students that posts intro slides of admitted students. Submit yours with a real photo, your major, your hometown, and one specific interest. "Love music" gets scrolled past. "Looking for someone to go to every Iowa volleyball game with" gets DMs.

2. Comment on other people's posts first

You do not have to wait to be found. Comment on posts from people in your major or your hall preference, then follow up in DMs. Most people are relieved someone else made the first move.

3. Join the group chats, then leave the bad ones

Class group chats are chaotic in spring. Stay for the smaller spinoffs: your major, your hall, your interests. A 30-person chat where people actually talk beats a 900-person chat nobody reads.

4. Use your roommate search as a friend search

Talking to potential roommates is meeting people, even the ones you do not end up rooming with. Many of the friends students make before college come straight out of the roommate hunt. Our University of Iowa roommate finder guide covers where to look.

5. Find your floor before you find your room

Once hall assignments come out, look for people in the same building. In a big social hall like Burge, with 922 residents, you will meet people anyway. In a smaller hall like Daum or Stanley, reaching out before move-in matters more. Not sure which hall fits you? See our University of Iowa dorms ranked list.

6. Move from DMs to real plans

Online friends only turn into real friends when you make a plan. Pick something concrete for the first week: a dining hall meal, the first home football game, a Welcome Week event. Text it the week before so it actually happens.

If it feels awkward, that is normal. Everyone in your class is new at the same time. The people who seem to know everyone on day one usually just sent a few more messages in July.

A month-by-month plan for making friends before college

  1. Right after you are admittedFollow your class page and submit an intro post.
  2. SpringJoin two or three small group chats and start the roommate search.
  3. When hall assignments come outFind people in your building and on your floor.
  4. JulyMove your best conversations to a call or a meetup if you live nearby.
  5. The week before move-inLock in one first-week plan with people you met online.

What not to stress about

The people you meet before college will not all be your people, and that is fine. Most close friendships form in the first months on campus, through your floor, your classes, and the clubs you join. Making a few connections before move-in just means your first week starts with familiar faces instead of none.

Frequently asked questions

How do you make friends before college starts?

Post a specific intro on your class Instagram page, comment on other students' posts, join a few small group chats, and turn the best conversations into a concrete plan for your first week on campus.

Is it normal to not have friends before college?

Yes. Most incoming freshmen arrive knowing only a few people, if any, and most close college friendships form in the first months on campus.

Should I join my class group chat?

Join it, but spend your time in the smaller spinoff chats for your major, hall, or interests, where people actually talk.

What is the most social dorm at Iowa?

Burge Hall, with 922 residents and a dining hall in the building, is the easiest place to meet a lot of people quickly.
