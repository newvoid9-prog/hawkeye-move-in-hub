From admitted to moved in, in six steps

The order matters. Students who pick a roommate after their hall preferences lock often end up wherever there is space.

  1. Accept your admission and apply for housingThe housing application opens after you are admitted and comes with a $100 non-refundable fee. Applying early gives you the best shot at your top halls.
  2. Decide: roommate or randomFind someone through the roommate search or class pages, or choose random on purpose. Both work. Deciding late does not.
  3. Rank your halls togetherIf you have a roommate, agree on your top three halls before either of you submits preferences.
  4. Pick a meal plan you will actually useMost first-years must choose Unlimited, Weekly 14, or Block 160. Rooms with a university kitchen unlock the cheaper Block 50.
  5. Meet your floor before you meet your roomOnce assignments come out, find people in your building on the class pages and group chats.
  6. Make a first-week planOne concrete plan with people you met online turns move-in week from scary to familiar.

Who runs Hawkeye Move-In Hub

Hawkeye Move-In Hub started in a Burge study lounge when two sophomores, Maya Lindqvist and Desmond Okafor, realized they had both spent their senior spring refreshing the same five group chats trying to figure out Iowa housing. They built the guide they wished they had: one place for the roommate search, the hall comparison, and the cost math, written by students instead of a brochure.

Every number on this site comes from the published 2026-27 University Housing and Dining rate sheet and hall listings. Every opinion, like which hall is best, is ours, and we say so.

The short version: apply for housing early, pick a roommate or decide to go random before your hall preferences lock, and budget for room plus a required meal plan, not just the room. A standard double plus the cheapest required plan is $13,174 for the year.
