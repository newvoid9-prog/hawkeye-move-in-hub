University of Iowa meal plan costs

If your room does not have a university-supplied kitchen, you must pick Unlimited, Weekly 14, or Block 160. Swipes work at the three all-you-care-to-eat dining halls: Burge, Catlett, and Hillcrest Market Places. Students in rooms with a kitchen, like the Mayflower suites, can also choose Block 50.

Which meal plan should you pick?

  • Unlimited if you live in Burge, Catlett, or Hillcrest and will eat in the dining hall most days. It is only $410 more than Block 160.
  • Block 160 if you skip breakfast or eat off campus some days. That is about ten swipes a week.
  • Weekly 14 if you like a steady two meals a day and would waste a block.
  • Block 50 only if your room has a kitchen and you will really cook.

Unused dining dollars carry over from fall to spring, but expire at the end of spring semester.

Costs people forget

  • A $100 non-refundable housing application fee.
  • Laundry beyond the included $120: $1.75 per wash and $1.25 per dry.
  • Winter break housing: a flat $350 if you stay on campus.
  • Early move-in: $35 per day if you are approved to arrive early.
