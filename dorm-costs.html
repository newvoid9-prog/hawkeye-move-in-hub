Budgeting

University of Iowa dorm costs for 2026-27, by hall and room type

Your housing bill is two charges, not one: the room and a required meal plan. Here is what each costs this year and what a full year really adds up to.

Room rates by room type

Rates are per person for the full academic year, including fall and spring breaks. Every room comes furnished and air conditioned, with utilities, internet, and Wi-Fi included. Each rate below also includes $120 in laundry dollars.

What a full year actually costs

  • Cheapest realistic setup: Hillcrest quad plus Block 160 comes to $10,885 a year.
  • Most common setup: a standard double plus Block 160 comes to $13,174 a year.
  • The premium setup: a Catlett or Petersen double plus Unlimited comes to $14,883 a year.

The gap between a standard double and a Catlett or Petersen double is $1,299 a year. Whether that is worth it depends on how much the hall itself matters to you. Our Iowa dorms ranked list can help you decide.

Costs people forget

  • A $100 non-refundable housing application fee.
  • Laundry beyond the included $120: $1.75 per wash and $1.25 per dry.
  • Winter break housing, a flat $350 if you stay on campus.
  • Early move-in, charged at $35 per day if you are approved to arrive early.

Split costs with your roommate early. Decide who brings the fridge, microwave, and rug before move-in. Still looking for one? Start with our University of Iowa roommate finder guide.
